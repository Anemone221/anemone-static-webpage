    MCO: Massive Character Organization

    A desktop app for EVE players who run a lot of characters. It works for 10 or more and is designed around 90+. It tracks the whole roster outside the game: who's training what, which account is idle, who can fly a given fit, where everyone is parked, which clones hold which implants, and which blueprints you own.

    • Fits: paste an EFT fit to see how many of your characters can fly it and what the rest are missing.
    • Location: find the cyno alt nearest to a system, by gate jumps or light years, with jump clones included.
    • Accounts: get warned when an Omega account has nobody training.
    • Local-only: everything lives in one SQLite file on your machine. There's no server, no account, and nothing is uploaded.

    Windows, macOS, and Linux · Free and open source (MIT) · Unofficial and not affiliated with CCP.
